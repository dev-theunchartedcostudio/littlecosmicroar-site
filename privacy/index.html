Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 2025  ·  Developer: The Uncharted Co.

Heirloom is designed from the ground up to keep your family's memories private.

Data We Do Not Collect

The Uncharted Co. does not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information on external servers. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking frameworks. We do not sell, share, or monetize your data in any form.

Data Stored on Your Device

All memories, photos, family tree information, growth data, recipes, prompts, and notes are stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage frameworks. This data belongs to you and remains under your control at all times.

iCloud / CloudKit Sync

If you choose to enable iCloud sync, your data is synchronized through Apple's CloudKit infrastructure. This sync is governed by Apple's iCloud privacy policy and is encrypted in transit and at rest. The Uncharted Co. does not have access to data stored in your iCloud account.

Share Extension

The Heirloom share extension allows you to import content (photos, text, links) from other apps on your device into the app. Content shared through this extension is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to any external service.

Camera and Photo Library

Heirloom requests access to your camera and photo library solely to allow you to capture and import photos into your memories. We do not transmit these images anywhere.

Notifications

If you enable reminders or notifications, scheduling is performed entirely on-device using Apple's local notification system. We do not process notification data externally.

Children's Privacy

Heirloom is designed to help families preserve memories about children. We do not knowingly collect any data from children, nor do we collect any data at all through our own servers.

Contact

If you have questions about privacy, please contact us at hello@theunchartedco.studio. We are happy to assist.

This policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the current policy.

In-App Purchases & Premium

Purchase Options

Heirloom offers optional premium features through the Apple App Store. Three purchase options are available: a monthly subscription, an annual subscription, and a one-time lifetime purchase. Premium features may include unlimited family tree members, CDC growth chart percentiles, heirloom book generation, and other capabilities as described within the App.

Subscriptions

Monthly and annual subscriptions grant access to all current and future Pro features for the duration of the subscription. Subscriptions auto-renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through your Apple ID account settings.

Lifetime Purchase

The lifetime purchase is a one-time payment that grants permanent access to all Pro features available at the time of purchase. New features added after your purchase date may be made available to subscription holders first and are not automatically included in the lifetime purchase.

Billing

All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The Developer does not have access to your payment information. All billing inquiries, refund requests, and subscription management should be directed to Apple through your App Store account settings.

Refunds

Refund requests are handled by Apple in accordance with the App Store refund policy. The Developer cannot process refunds directly. To request a refund, visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Offer Codes

The Developer may issue offer codes or promotional codes that unlock premium features. These codes are single-use and non-transferable. The Developer reserves the right to modify or revoke offer codes at any time.

Future Changes

The Developer reserves the right to add, modify, or remove features associated with premium access. Existing purchasers will be notified of material changes to what their purchase includes.
